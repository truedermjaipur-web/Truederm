Carbon Facial & Chemical Peels

Carbon laser facials and chemical peels are popular for their quick turnaround - minimal downtime with a visible improvement in texture, tone and clarity. Peels are selected and layered carefully based on your skin's sensitivity, so you get results without unnecessary irritation.

Vitiligo Treatment

Vitiligo is an autoimmune-related condition where skin loses pigment in patches. Dr. Khan takes a realistic, evidence-based approach - assessing the extent and activity of the condition before recommending topical therapy, phototherapy referral, or camouflage options, with honest expectations set from the first consultation.

Keloid & Scar Treatment

Keloids and thickened scars are managed with intralesional injections, silicone-based therapy and, where appropriate, combination approaches - planned around the scar's size, age and location, with regular follow-up since keloids can be prone to recurrence.

FAQs

Common Questions

What does a carbon laser facial actually do?

A thin layer of carbon is applied to the skin and targeted with a laser, which helps refine pores, remove surface impurities and leave skin looking brighter. It's a quick, low-downtime procedure often used for an instant glow before an event.

Are chemical peels safe for Indian skin?

Yes, when the right type and strength of peel is chosen for your skin type. Dr. Khan selects the peel and its concentration based on your skin's sensitivity to avoid irritation or post-treatment pigmentation.

Can vitiligo be cured completely?

Vitiligo is a chronic condition, and no treatment can guarantee complete, permanent repigmentation for every patient. Dr. Khan focuses on realistic, medically appropriate management - which may include topical treatment, phototherapy referral or camouflage options - to stabilise the condition and improve appearance.

Do keloids come back after treatment?

Keloids can recur, which is why treatment is usually a combination approach - such as intralesional injections and silicone therapy - with follow-up monitoring rather than a single procedure.
