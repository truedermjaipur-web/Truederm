Dermal Fillers in Jaipur: A Beginner's Guide to Natural Results

By Dr. Iftekhar Khan, MBBS, MD Dermatology (AIIMS Delhi) · Truederm, C-Scheme, Jaipur
Inside the consultation room at Truederm, C-Scheme, Jaipur

Dermal fillers have quietly become one of the most requested treatments at Truederm - not for a dramatically different look, but for that "did you get some sleep?" freshness patients are actually after. If you're considering fillers for the first time, here's what's worth knowing before you book a consultation.

What fillers actually do

Most fillers used at Truederm are hyaluronic acid-based - a substance your skin already produces naturally. Injected in precise, small amounts, it restores lost volume, softens lines, and can subtly reshape areas like the cheeks, jawline or lips. It is not a permanent change; results are designed to look natural and settle in over roughly two weeks.

Where fillers are commonly used

  • Lips - added definition and volume, kept proportional to your face
  • Cheeks - restoring volume lost with age for a softer, lifted look
  • Jawline & chin - subtle contouring and balance
  • Under-eyes (tear trough) - softening hollows that make you look tired
  • Nasolabial folds - softening the lines that run from nose to mouth corners

The "overfilled" look - and how to avoid it

The pillow-face effect patients worry about almost always comes from too much filler placed without a plan for facial proportion, not from fillers themselves. Dr. Khan's approach starts with an honest conversation about what will actually look right on your face - sometimes that means using less than a patient initially expects, or spacing sessions out rather than doing everything at once.

A good sign in a clinic: if a dermatologist is willing to tell you "less is more" or suggest waiting before adding more filler, that's usually a sign they're optimising for how you'll look in daily life - not for the treatment room mirror.

What the appointment actually involves

A numbing cream is applied first, so most of what you feel is light pressure rather than pain. The injection itself takes a matter of minutes per area. Mild swelling or a small bruise at the injection site is common and usually settles within a few days.

How long do results last?

This varies by area and product - lips typically need touch-ups sooner (around 6-9 months) while cheek and jawline fillers can last 12-18 months. Everybody metabolises filler at a slightly different rate, which is one of the things discussed at your consultation.

Questions worth asking before you book anywhere

  • Is the product FDA/CDSCO-approved and from a reputable brand?
  • Who is actually performing the injection - a doctor, or someone else?
  • Can the filler be dissolved if I'm not happy with it?
