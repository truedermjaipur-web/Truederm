Under-Eye Care

Why do dark circles happen?

Dark circles are not one problem. They can come from pigmentation (brown discolouration), hollowness under the eyes that casts a shadow, thin skin that shows the blood vessels beneath, or a mix of these. Lack of sleep, allergies, rubbing the eyes, genetics, ageing and sun exposure can all make them worse. Creams alone often fail because they don't match the cause.

Treatments we use

  • Pigmented dark circles: prescription creams, gentle chemical peels and lasers where suitable
  • Hollow or tired-looking under-eyes: hyaluronic acid under-eye fillers
  • Thin skin and fine lines: skin-strengthening treatments and good skincare
  • Allergy-related darkening: treating the allergy and stopping eye rubbing
  • Lifestyle and sun-protection advice to keep results lasting

Why see a dermatologist?

The skin under the eyes is very thin and easily irritated. Choosing the wrong peel or product can make pigmentation worse. A dermatologist can tell which type of dark circles you have and choose treatments that are safe for this area.

FAQs

Common Questions

Can dark circles be removed permanently?

They can usually be reduced a lot, but results depend on the cause. Genetic and age-related dark circles may need occasional maintenance.

Are under-eye fillers safe?

In trained hands, hyaluronic acid fillers are a well-established treatment for under-eye hollows. Dr. Khan assesses whether you are a suitable candidate first.

How soon will I see results?

Fillers show an immediate difference. Peels and creams for pigmentation usually take several weeks.
