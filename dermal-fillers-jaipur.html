How Dermal Fillers Work

Most fillers used at Truederm are made of hyaluronic acid, a substance naturally found in your skin. Injected in carefully measured amounts, it adds volume and structure exactly where it's needed, with results visible immediately and continuing to settle over the following two weeks.

What to Expect

  • A detailed facial assessment to understand your goals and natural proportions
  • Numbing cream applied before injection for comfort
  • Precise, measured injection technique - never overfilled
  • Immediate visible results, with final settling over 1-2 weeks
  • A follow-up review to confirm you're happy with the outcome

FAQs

Common Questions

How long do dermal fillers last?

Most hyaluronic acid fillers last anywhere from 6 to 18 months depending on the area treated, the product used, and your individual metabolism. Lips tend to soften sooner than cheeks or jawline fillers.

Is getting fillers painful?

A numbing cream is applied before injection, so most patients feel only mild pressure or a slight pinch. Discomfort is usually minimal and short-lived.

Can dermal fillers be reversed?

Yes. Hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved with an enzyme if you're unhappy with the result or want to make an adjustment, which is one reason they're considered a safer starting point than permanent fillers.

Is there any downtime after filler treatment?

Most patients return to normal activities the same day. Mild swelling, tenderness or minor bruising at the injection site can occur and typically settles within a few days.
