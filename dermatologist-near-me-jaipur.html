Skin, hair and nail conditions: acne and acne scars, pigmentation and melasma, eczema and allergies, fungal infections, vitiligo, hair fall and baldness, moles, warts and skin tags. Dermatologists also do procedures such as lasers, chemical peels, Botox, fillers and hair transplant.
