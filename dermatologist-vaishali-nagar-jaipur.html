Common treatments for patients from Vaishali Nagar

Acne and acne scars

Young professionals and college students from Vaishali Nagar often visit for active acne and the marks it leaves. Treatment starts with finding the cause, then combining medicines with peels, microneedling or laser for scars.

Hair fall and thinning

Stress, diet, thyroid and hormones are common drivers. Dr. Khan evaluates the cause first and then plans medicines, PRP or, where suitable, FUE/DHT hair transplant.

Pigmentation and tanning

Daily commutes in Jaipur's sun lead to tanning, melasma and dark spots. We treat with prescription creams, chemical peels and laser, alongside a sunscreen routine.
